# Styles d'image

Chaque section `## nom` est un style, envoyé tel quel à n8n après la scène.
La section `identite` (le verrou d'identité) est ajoutée à chaque appel, quel que soit le style. Elle ne décrit pas son visage : c'est l'Image 1 qui le porte.
Pour ajouter un style : une nouvelle section `## nom-court`, en anglais (les modèles d'image suivent mieux).

## identite
Identity lock. Image 1 is the main reference: a real photo of this real man. The other reference images show the same man from other angles; use them only to confirm his identity.
- Keep exactly, with zero deviation from the reference photos: face shape, eyes, eyebrows, nose, mouth, ears, forehead and hairline, beard (length, density, shape, colour), hair tied back, skin tone and skin texture
- He must be instantly recognisable by people who know him: do not beautify, slim, smooth, rejuvenate or symmetrise his face
- Take only his identity from the references: ignore their clothes, caps, sunglasses, backgrounds and lighting
- No text, no logos, no readable code anywhere

## fusain
Traditional charcoal drawing on textured off-white paper, hand-drawn look.
- Expressive charcoal strokes, cross-hatching for shadows, smudged soft gradients
- Black and grey only, no colour
- The person is drawn with care and likeness; the background is sketched with fewer, lighter strokes
- Lots of empty paper around the subject, unfinished edges like a sketchbook page

## crayon
Graphite pencil sketch on white paper, light and precise.
- Fine pencil lines, light shading, visible construction lines in the background
- Grey tones only
- Clean, airy composition, the person sharp, the setting barely suggested

## photo-iphone
Candid real photo taken by a friend on an iPhone 16 Pro, main camera at 2x (about 48mm equivalent), Portrait mode with gentle natural background blur.
- Natural available light only (window light, daylight, the room's own lamps), no studio light, no rim light, no dramatic shadows
- Typical smartphone processing: slightly bright exposure, mild HDR, faint sensor noise in the shadows, true-to-life colours, no filter, no colour grading
- Real skin with visible pores, small imperfections and natural redness, fine individual beard hairs, slightly flyaway hair: no retouching, no airbrushing, no plastic skin
- Small real-life imperfections: slightly off-centre framing, a little motion blur in the background only, everyday clutter left as it is
- The person fills 50-60% of the frame, the background stays recognisable
- Photorealistic: no illustration, no painting, no cinematic look. If it looks too perfect, too symmetrical or too professional, it is wrong

## portrait-profil
Realistic LinkedIn profile headshot, shot on a Sony A7 IV with an 85mm f/1.8 lens, camera at eye level.
- Photorealistic: real skin texture, visible pores, fine beard hairs, no retouching, no airbrushed or plastic skin, no AI-perfect symmetry, no illustration
- Soft diffused key light from one side (large softbox) with a gentle fill, soft catchlights in the eyes, no harsh shadows, no rim light
- Tight head-and-shoulders crop from mid-chest up: the head and beard fill about 60% of the frame, eyes about 40% down from the top, small headroom, nothing important near the corners (the photo will be cropped to a circle)
- Plain solid seamless studio backdrop (the colour given in the scene, default muted slate blue-grey), evenly lit. Absolutely no window, no wall edge, no room, no furniture, no objects
- Face toward the camera, shoulders turned slightly, looking straight into the lens with his own natural relaxed smile
